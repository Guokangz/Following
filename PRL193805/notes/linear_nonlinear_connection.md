## Derivation

For a 2LS initially in `|g><g|`, use the PRL/SM positive-frequency RWA equations in a frame rotating at the CW drive frequency `omega`:

`dot alpha = -[kappa/2 + i(omega_c-omega)] alpha - i G rho_eg - u`,

`dot rho_eg = -[Gamma_2 + i(omega_0-omega)] rho_eg - i G alpha`,

where the cavity field has been scaled by `sqrt(N)`, `G=g sqrt(N)`, and `u` is the correspondingly scaled incident drive. The physical ratio `alpha/input` is unchanged by this scaling.

The SM coherence rate is

`Gamma_2 = (gamma_population + gamma_phi)/2`.

At steady state,

`alpha/u = -1 / {kappa/2 + i(omega_c-omega) + G^2/[Gamma_2+i(omega_0-omega)]}`.

The linear-paper susceptibility is

`chi(omega) = -G^2/[omega-omega_0+i gamma_linear/2]`,

and the photon Green function is

`D^R(omega) = 1/[omega-omega_c+i kappa/2+chi(omega)]`.

They are identical under

`gamma_linear = gamma_population + gamma_phi`

and

`D^R = i alpha/u`.

The factor `i` is an overall phase caused by the PRL cavity equation's explicit `-input` convention. It disappears from transmission, which depends on the modulus squared. No linewidth or coupling parameter is adjusted.

## Numerical comparison

Parameters:

- `omega_c=omega_0=0` as the frequency origin;
- `kappa=0.1`;
- `G=2`;
- mean-field `gamma_population=0.1`, `gamma_phi=0.2`;
- therefore linear-paper `gamma_linear=0.3`;
- balanced cavity `kappa_L=kappa_R=kappa/2`.

Over a 20001-point frequency grid on `[-5,5]`:

- maximum complex difference between `D^R` and `i alpha/u`: `0` at floating-point precision;
- maximum transmission difference: `0` at floating-point precision.

An independent propagation of the full nonlinear mean-field ODE was performed for a constant scaled drive amplitude `u=1e-6` at drive frequencies `-2,0,+2`. After 800 time units:

- maximum full-ODE response difference from the first-order steady solution: `7.51e-9`;
- maximum trace error: `0` (the two populations are represented as `p` and `1-p`);
- minimum density-matrix eigenvalue: `0` within reported precision.

The test suite additionally verifies:

- zero drive plus ground state plus empty cavity remains exactly stationary;
- the weak-CW full nonlinear solution converges to the linear response;
- the resonant peak splitting is `approximately 2G` under weak damping;
- `H0` and the transition dipole operator are Hermitian;
- population remains bounded and the reconstructed 2LS density matrix remains positive in the weak-field checks.

## Convention checklist for later nonlinear stages

1. Keep the cavity amplitude decay as `kappa/2`; do not replace it by `kappa`.
2. Keep the SM molecular coherence decay as `(gamma+gamma_phi)/2`.
3. Use `gamma_linear=gamma+gamma_phi` when comparing to Eq. (34)/(38).
4. In the rotating frame, detunings in the ODE are `omega_c-omega_drive` and `omega_0-omega_drive`.
5. Preserve the PRL `-input` sign; compensate only when comparing complex Green functions, not by changing the drive.
6. Use `kappa_L=kappa_R=kappa/2` for the balanced-cavity transmission normalization in SM Eq. (S.6).
7. Validate any FFT convention against the CW result and SM Gaussian transform Eq. (S.20) before computing pump-probe spectra.
